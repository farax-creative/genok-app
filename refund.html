Refund Policy

Effective 2026-09-30

Payment is not yet available. Until integration with a payment processor is complete, no paid transactions take place, so there are no transactions eligible for a refund either. The criteria below apply from the time payment opens.

1. Check it for free first

Genok can be used for free through one entire loop, and you can try the paid features for 14 days from the day you start the trial (once). Before buying, you can fully check whether the App suits your own environment.

2. Subscription (Pro) refunds

  • If you have not used the paid features after payment, you may withdraw your order and receive a full refund within 7 days of the payment date (in accordance with the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).
  • If you cancel a monthly subscription before the next billing date, no further fees are charged. A usage period that has already started can be used until the end of the remaining period.
  • For an annual subscription, once the withdrawal period above has passed, a refund is considered on the basis of the remaining period after deducting the period already used.

3. Lifetime plan refunds

For the Lifetime plan, you may request a refund within 14 days of the purchase date. The refund proceeds if the license key has not been reused elsewhere and is properly retrieved.

4. When a refund is difficult

  • Where the case is not one of the user's mistake or a problem with the user's own environment, but one where the user has already used the App for a considerable period and simply changed their mind after the withdrawal period passed.
  • Where the Terms of Service have been violated, for example by reselling or sharing the license key.

However, where normal use is impossible because of a serious defect in the App, we will provide a refund or help resolve the problem regardless of the period.

5. How refunds are made

Refunds are processed to the payment method used, through the payment processor. After the processor is integrated, the specific way to apply will be stated in this section. Until then, please inquire at the contact below.
